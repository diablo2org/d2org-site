# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is people exploring the legacy Diablo II ecosystem today. This includes returning players choosing how to play, players comparing active mods and servers, modders looking for reliable technical references, and readers researching community history.

## Product Purpose

diablo2.org is a community directory and knowledge base for the original Diablo II and Lord of Destruction. It helps people find viable ways to play, compare projects and tools, understand version compatibility, and trace claims back to sources. Success means a reader can make a practical choice without piecing together old forum posts, Discord messages, and dead links.

## Positioning

The site joins structured, version-aware project records with maintained guides and historical context. It remains neutral across vanilla play, mods, servers, and community projects.

## Operating Context

Most visits begin with a concrete question from search or a returning player's need to understand what still works. Readers scan project status, compatibility, platform support, source links, and last-verified dates before opening detailed guides or external project pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content lives in repository-managed MDX and TypeScript data.
- Search covers guides, projects, tools, mechanics, technical material, and history.
- Structured relationships connect projects, versions, tools, and guides.
- Facts must be sourced or verified. Unknown fields stay absent rather than being guessed.
- The product must remain useful without accounts or community features.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is diablo2.org. Writing is plain, specific, neutral across projects, and uses sentence-case headings. The visual identity may change completely while content, routes, and product behavior remain intact.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

- Help readers compare before asking them to read deeply.
- Show compatibility, status, provenance, and uncertainty where decisions are made.
- Treat legacy knowledge as maintained reference material, not nostalgia alone.
- Keep project coverage factual and neutral.
- Make the dense ecosystem easy to scan without reducing it to a generic wiki.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, responsive layouts, readable text contrast, and reduced-motion preferences.
