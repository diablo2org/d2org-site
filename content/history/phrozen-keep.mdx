If you've modded Diablo II, you've used [the Phrozen Keep](https://d2mods.info) whether you knew it or not. Most of what the community knows about the game's files was worked out, argued over and written down in its forums and knowledge base. It has been running since 2000, which makes it older than Lord of Destruction.

<Callout type="tip" title="Start here if you're modding">
The Keep's [Knowledge Base](https://d2mods.info/forum/kb/index) holds the file guides and tutorials. Its [forums](https://d2mods.info/forum/index.php) are still where modders ask questions and post their projects.
</Callout>

## Origins, 2000

The Keep began in June 2000 as a Diablo I item repository on Tripod, run by a player called Phrozen Heart. A month later, after finding Rana Loreus's mod site The Forge, Phrozen Heart learned to mod Diablo II and rebuilt the Keep as a central archive for Diablo II modding resources.

Iowan joined in October 2000 and became a permanent co-webmaster. The Phrozen Forums opened in November 2000. In December, after the mods section of Diabloii.net closed, Fusman joined as a third co-webmaster and brought that site's audience along.

## Growing up, 2001 to 2004

In March 2001 the Keep joined the Planet Diablo network at GameSpy, which gave it free hosting and bandwidth. Two patches then defined its next few years:

- **1.09d** (November 2001) became the modders' platform for the next couple of years.
- **1.10** (October 2003) moved much more of the game's data into TXT files. That made far more of the game moddable without code.

Two releases from this period are still in use. In March 2004 Yohann introduced [PlugY](/tools/plugy), which removed the game's storage limits. In May 2004 SVR released D2Mod, a code plugin system that let non-programmers extend their mods.

## The realm schism, 2004

In December 2004, after the Davidson & Associates v. Internet Gateway court decision, the Keep banned all discussion of unofficial Diablo II realms. The policy split the membership. It's a useful reminder of how uncertain private servers looked at the time.

## Hosting changes, 2005 to 2009

Patch 1.11 arrived in August 2005, after Blizzard North closed, and restructured much of the game code that modders relied on. The founder, Phrozen Heart, retired in June 2006. Onyx became senior admin in January 2009.

When GameSpy ended its hosting in August 2009, Onyx rebuilt the site from scratch on phpBB 3. In December 2009 FilePlanet deleted the Keep's hosted files without warning. Onyx and Volf moved everything to new hosting.

## The Keep today

The Keep still focuses on legacy Diablo II modding, and it now also covers Diablo II Resurrected. Beyond the forums and knowledge base, it maintains open-source projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep):

- **[D2MOO](/tools/d2moo)** is a reimplementation of the game's code, based on 1.10f. It's the best open reference for how the game actually works.
- **[D2Template](/tools/d2template)** is a starter project for injecting your own code into the game.
- **CE_Database** is a database for code editing.
