This is a list of 50 of the most notable  
characters from the Lord of the Rings trilogy which could prove useful  
for naming everything from magical affixes to your new boss monsters.

Please credit the Phrozen Keep ([http://www.d2mods.info](http://www.d2mods.info)) and J.R.R. Tolkien in the readme file for your mod if you find this list useful. Thanks.

Aldor - Second son of Brego and longest reigning king of Rohan

Aragorn Elessar - Ranger, Husband of Arwen and King of Gondor

Arwen Evenstar - Daughter of Elrond and wife of Aragorn

Barliman Butterbur - Innkeeper of the Prancing Pony where Aragorn and Frodo first meet

Bereg - Great-grandson of Bëor the Old, Leader of the rebels of the House of Bëor

Bilbo Baggins - Hobbit adventurer who first passes the ring to his adopted son Frodo

Boromir - Human member of the Fellowship and brother of Faramir and son of Denethor

Celeborn - Lord of Lórien, husband of Galadriel and ruler of the Galadhrim

Damrod - One of Faramir's Rangers of Ithilien

Déagol - Hobbit-like finder of the One Ring who was murdered by his friend Sméagol

Denethor - Steward of Gondor and father of Boromir and Faramir

Elanor Gardner - Eldest child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton

Eldarion - Son of Aragorn II Elessar and Arwen Evenstar

Elendil - Last Lord of Andúnië and founder of the kingdoms of Arnor and Gondor

Elrond - Half-elf founder and Lord of Rivendell and Father of Arwen

Éomer Éadig - First of the Third Line of the Kings of Rohan and nephew of King Théoden

Éowyn - Shieldmaiden of Rohan, Daughter of King Théoden and wife of Faramir

Everard Proudfoot - Distant cousin of Bilbo and Frodo Baggins, Peregrin Took and Meriadoc Brandybuck

Faramir - Prince of Ithilien, Lord of Emyn Arnen and husband of Éowyn

Farmer Maggot - Hobbit who farmed lands in the Marish, in the Eastfarthing of the Shire

Frodo Baggins - Hobbit member of the Fellowship and possesser of the One Ring

Galadriel - Lady of Lórien, wife of Celeborn and leader of the Galadhrim

Gamling - Leader of the watchers of Helm's Dike, part of Helm's Deep.

Gandalf the Grey/Gandalf the White - Human mage member of the Felllowship and resurrected as the white wizard

Gimli Elf-friend - Dwarven member of the Fellowship and son of Glóin

Gollum/Sméagol - Schitzophrenic remains of a hobbit-like creature corrupted by the One Ring

Gorbag - Uruk of Minas Morgul, slain by Shagrat in the Tower of Cirith Ungol

Gothmog - High-captain of Angband, Lord of Balrogs

Grima Wormtongue - Counsellor of Théoden King of Rohan, who acted as a spy of Saruman

Grimbold of Westfold - Captain of the Riders of Rohirrim

Grishnákh - Orc of Mordor who was part of the raiding party that captured Merry and Pippin

Haldir - Elf of Lórien belonging to the Galadhrim (Silvan Elves of Lórien)

Háma - Doorward of Théoden and captain of the King's guard of Rohan

Isildur - King of Gondor who cut the One Ring from the hand of Sauron

King of the Dead - Leader of the ghostly exiles in the Cursed Mountains

Legolas Greenleaf - Elven member of the Fellowship and son of Thranduil, Lord of the Elves of Mirkwood

Lothíriel - Daughter of Prince Imrahil of Dol Amroth and wife of King Éomer of Rohan

Mauhúr - Orc of Isengard in the service of Saruman

Meriadoc 'Merry' Brandybuck - Hobbit member of the Fellowship who killed the Witch-king of Angmar with Éowyn

Peregrin 'Pippin' Took - Hobbit member of the Fellowship and former bondsman of Denethor II of Gondor

Rosie Cotton - Wife of Samwise Gamgee

Samwise 'Sam' Gamgee - Hobbit member of the Fellowship, gardener of Frodo Baggins and husband of Rosie Cotton

Saruman - White Wizard who was first of the 5 Wizards to arrive in Middle-Earth corrupted to become Saruman of Many Colors

Sauron - Originally a Maia of Aulë's people corrupted by Melkor and became his most trusted lieutenant

Shagrat - The Uruk commanding the Tower of Cirith Ungol in the Ephel Dúath

Théoden - Son of Thengel and the seventeenth King of Rohan succeeded by his nephew, Éomer

Théodred - Only son and heir of King Théoden of Rohan who was slain in the First Battle of the Fords of Isen

Fangorn/Treebeard - Old Ent who inhabited the great Forest at the southern feet of the Misty Mountains

Uglúk - Uruk in the service of Saruman, the leader of the orc-band that captured Merry and Pippin

White Witch King of Agmar - Lord and mightiest of the Nazgûl, the Nine Servants of Sauron slain by Éowyn and Merry
