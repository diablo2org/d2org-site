Sets are always a challenge, mostly because of how easy it is to mess up when working with the setitems.txt file and putting the names of your set and your set items in a .tbl file.  

But, coming up with names and ideas is no cakewalk either. Here is a list of ideas for your consideration. Hopefully they will work well for your mod or will inspire you to come up with your own ideas.  

Please credit Phrozen Keep ([http://www.d2mods.com](http://www.d2mods.com)) in the readme file for your mod if you choose and use any names from this list. Thanks.
