Here's a resource on which number to put in the param column when you are using the 'skilltab' modifier.

Skilltab numbers :

Amazon  
0 - Bow & Crossbow  
1 - Passive & Magic  
2 - Spear & Javelin

Sorceress  
3 - Fire  
4 - Lightning  
5 - Cold

Necromancer  
6 - Curses  
7 - Poison & Bone  
8 - Summoning

Paladin  
9 - Offensive Auras  
10 - Combat Skills  
11 - Defensive Auras

Barbarian  
12 - Masteries  
13 - Combat Skills  
14 - Warcries

Druid  
15 - Summoning  
16 - Shapeshifting  
17 - Elemental

Assassin  
18 - Traps  
19 - Shadow Disciplines  
20 - Martial Arts
