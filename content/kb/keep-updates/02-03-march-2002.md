**Nude Assassin Mod**  
At one point, no one thought this was possible and with various urban legends floating round about a naked assassin or sorceress mod, it came as a mildly amusing suprise to come across this impressive creation. While on one note, seeing a bare-chested killing machine running round disembowelling demons is just a simple novelty but on the flip side it shows that D2 mod making is still evolving with people now actually editing their own animations rather than just converting. Any cool animations are always impressive but usually original ones are more so. The mod in question was created by someone by the name of Blue Orange and to get your hands on it, you need to download the 6 pieces of the compressed RAR archive ( [1](http://www.threshed.com/d2/NudeAssassinFull.part1.rar) | [2](http://www.threshed.com/d2/NudeAssassinFull.part2.rar) | [3](http://www.threshed.com/d2/NudeAssassinFull.part3.rar) | [4](http://www.threshed.com/d2/NudeAssassinFull.part4.rar) | [5](http://www.threshed.com/d2/NudeAssassinFull.part5.rar) | [6](http://www.threshed.com/d2/NudeAssassinFull.part6.rar) ). **WinMPQ**  
Mod makers might be interested in checking out a mod utility that was recently brought to my attention by Fusman named WinMPQ. I haven't really had a chance to experiment with it yet but at first glance it appears to be a combination of MPQ View and MPQ 2K through a winzip looking windows interface. The utility was most likely developed for use with Starcraft but as Blizzard uses the same MPQ format uniformly across the board then there's no reason why we can't make the most of it. The utility requires the visual basic run time 4 files and a special direct x control but I've added both to the winmpq zip before I uploaded it to the Keep. [Click here](http://www.fileplanet.com/dl/dl.asp?/planetdiablo/phrozenkeep/031902/winmpq.zip) to download v1.47 from this site or you can check out the author Shadowflare's site by [clicking here](http://shadowflare.ancillaediting.net/).

**Round 2 to Bnetd?**  
Looks like the rebuttal to [Blizzard](http://www.blizzard.com/)'s "Intelectual Property" dispute with Bnetd and other server-emulation organizations by members of the LawMeme project at Yale Law School has had more of an impact than everyone previously thought with [Bnetd](http://www.bnetd.org/) re-opening their doors for the moment. This fight still seems to be leaning towards the [Blizzard](http://www.blizzard.com/) camp though due to them having the resources to conduct a lengthy court battle where [Bnetd](http://www.bnetd.org/) doesn't. For the full story directly from Bnet, [click here](http://www.bnetd.org/case_faq.php).
