| **DEATHSCYTHE** | Can you please tell us something about your self and how you come up with your unique nick? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | I don't really remember how I came up with my name except that it was during the first Diablo II. I do remember how I got the underscore ( \_ ), though. Many games at that time didn't allow you to use spaces in your name. The best substitute was an underscore. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | What do you do in real life? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | I'm a college student. You know, mod by day and party by night. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | And before modding the diablo 2, do you have any games you modified before modding diablo? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Well, the first thing I ever started modding was Sid Meier's Civilization II. I made a few things with that. After that, I did some light modding for Tribes. My first real serious mod was for Diablo II. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | When did you start making mods? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Well, I think my first mod was when I was around 13 years old, but I didn't start modding D2 until I was 18. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | How long do you already mod? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Since I'm all of 19 now, that makes it 6 years that I've been modding and about 1 year I've been modding D2. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | What part or feature in your mod you are proud of? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | I'm going to have to pick a feature from my next version and say the kill counter. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | How many weeks or months you make you first mod? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | The first version of my rune mod took about two weeks to make. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | Do you play also another mods? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | I try to when I get the chance. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | What is your favorite? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Probably the Baldur's Gate mod. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | What lines up in the future? Can you tell us what's were looking forward to? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Well, at the moment you can see a lot of this in my forum at the Next Version topic. The big highlights of my mod's next version is the kill counter, new magical attributes, a new interface, etc. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | Do you have any plans in making another mods or betas in the future? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Yes I do. I already have a few ideas I'm considering to improve my mod or perhaps to use in an entirely new mod. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | Do you have plans modifying the next blizzard patch? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Oh yes. I can't wait until it comes out. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | Is there any advice you'd give to anyone who's just starting out making mods or considering making a mod for D2? |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | Crashing Diablo II isn't all that bad. At least Diablo II is detecting the changes you made to your mod in order to crash. |
| **DEATHSCYTHE** | Thanks a lot for taking the time to answer our little interview :-) |
| **SIR\_GENERAL** | You're very welcome. |
