Please note that this tutorial no longer works with Diablo 2 1.08+ and the Expansion as the nomap column has been disabled.

I'm assuming in this tutorial that you know a bit about mod making and have read tutorial 1.  
To make monsters appear on the automap like in this [screenshot](http://www.planetdiablo.com/phrozenkeep/screens/showmap.jpg) just open up monstats.txt and find column J, it's called NOMAP, and it is set to 0 for all monsters. Just set it to 1 for all the monsters you want to show up on the automap. Save and add the monstats to your patch.mpq
