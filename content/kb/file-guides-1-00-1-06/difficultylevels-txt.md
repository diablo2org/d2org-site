Name : The name of the difficulty setting  
ResistPenalty : The percentage penalty to all resistances on this difficulty setting.  
DeathExpPenalty : The percentage of experience players lose when dying on this difficulty setting.  
UberCodeOddsNormal : The percentage chance that non-magical items found will be exceptional ones.  
UberCodeOddsGood : The percentage chance that magical or rare items found will be exceptional ones.  
MonsterSkillBonus : Bonus to monster skill levels on this difficulty setting.  
MonsterFreezeDivisor : The number that all freeze durations against monsters are divided by on this difficulty setting.  
MonsterColdDivisor : The number that all cold durations against monsters are divided by on this difficulty setting.  
AiCurseDivisor : The number that all curse durations against monsters are divided by on this difficulty setting.
